What the prototype demonstrates
1Time-of-flight loading
Home boots into shimmer skeletons while the portfolio hydrates asynchronously (~1.4s), then swaps to real balances. The shell, tab bar and Cora render instantly — the UI never blocks on the database.
2Live rate-lock countdown
Open Convert: the quote locks for 12s with a ticking timer, flips to “Rate expired”, then auto re-locks a fresh quote — a streamed value that can’t exist at page-load, driven by a client timer over a server quote.
3Optimistic settlement
Tap Convert instantly or Send: the button spins into a pending state immediately, a toast confirms, and balances update on settle (~1.6s) — the interaction feels instant while the backend settles out of band. Send toggles Instant vs ACH arrival copy.
Every value here is faked client-side purely to show the interaction contract. The mapping to real endpoints, cache, and websocket streams is documented in ARCHITECTURE.md.
