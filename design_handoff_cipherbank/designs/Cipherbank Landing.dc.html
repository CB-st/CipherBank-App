HOW IT WORKS

One balance. Every form of value.

01

Convert instantly

Swap crypto to cash — or cash to crypto — at a locked rate with zero conversion fees. Settlement is instant, not "3–5 business days."

02

Pay with a mix

Cover rent with Dogecoin, cash, and a slice of stock. We mediate the exchange in real time — the recipient just gets clean, settled funds.

03

Stay self-custodied

Your keys, your funds, your privacy. Shielded swaps and handle-based transfers mean counterparties see a handle — never you.

FOR BANKS & DEVELOPERS

The rails are open. Build on them.

Cipherbank is an open-source API standard for connecting financial systems. Banks embed crypto accounts and instant conversion directly into their own apps. Developers ship compliant, self-custodied money movement without reinventing the rails.

Embeddable UI — drop crypto account management into your existing app.
Open standard — one API contract across institutions, no lock-in.
Instant ACH + settlement — the same rails powering the consumer app.
